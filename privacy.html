Privacy

75 Days · last updated October 2026

Without an account, your data stays on your phone. Your habits, ticks, notes, weight and photos are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Photos never leave your phone, with or without an account.

The app does not use analytics, ads or tracking.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in uses Google Firebase. We store your email address and name, your habit list, start date, and each day's ticks, water, weight, energy and notes, so you can restore them on another phone. Only you can read this data.

Groups: when you join a group, its members can see your name, start date, and your daily scores (how many habits you completed), busy days and streak. They cannot see which habits, your notes, your weight or your photos.

Delete your account in Settings at any time. This removes your account, your backed-up days and your place in every group. Groups you created are deleted for everyone.

When you tap Share my progress, the app makes an image with your day, score and streak. It never includes your photos.

A backup file contains all your data, photos included. Keep it somewhere safe.
