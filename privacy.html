Privacy

75 Days · last updated October 2026

Your data stays on your phone. Your habits, ticks, notes, weight and photos are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account.

The app does not use analytics, ads, cookies for tracking, or third-party scripts.

When you tap Share my progress, the app makes an image with your day number, score and streak. It never includes your photos. You choose where to send it.

When you tap Save backup, the app creates a file with all your data, photos included. Keep it somewhere safe; anyone with that file can read it.

To delete everything, open Settings and tap Delete everything, or clear this site's data in your browser.
